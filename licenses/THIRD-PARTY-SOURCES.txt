Native libraries
  libmobi.so — libmobi, © Bartek Fabiszewski, LGPL-3.0-or-later.
    The exact bundled source is tracked at app/src/main/cpp/libmobi.
    Origin: https://github.com/bfabiszewski/libmobi
    The APK includes LGPL-3.0.txt and the GPL-3.0.txt that it supplements.
    It is linked dynamically by libnative-lib.so. Android's zlib is a
    platform library. libxml2 and libmobi encryption are disabled.
  libnative-lib.so — Android JNI bridge, app/src/main/cpp in this source tag.
    Statically includes:
    WOFF2 (MIT), app/src/main/cpp/woff2, https://github.com/google/woff2
    Brotli (MIT), app/src/main/cpp/woff2/brotli, https://github.com/google/brotli
    MD4C (MIT), app/src/main/cpp/md4c, https://github.com/mity/md4c
    The three original MIT texts are packaged with these notices.
  liboffice_oxide_bridge.so — JNI and Rust source: rust/office-oxide-bridge.
    office_oxide 0.1.13 (MIT OR Apache-2.0):
    https://crates.io/crates/office_oxide/0.1.13
    Exact transitive crate versions, registries and checksums: bundled
    OFFICE-OXIDE-Cargo.lock. Registry crate sources are available at
    https://static.crates.io/crates/<crate>/<crate>-<version>.crate
  libpdfiumandroid.so — io.legere:pdfiumandroid-core:2.0.0, Apache-2.0:
    https://github.com/johngray1965/PdfiumAndroidKt/tree/d298f0faa137cd2b9b0a913080c3d5374b3671f9
    libpdfium.so is provided as a prebuilt binary under
    pdfiumandroid/core/src/main/jniLibs in that tag. PDFium's source:
    https://pdfium.googlesource.com/pdfium/
    PDFium has a BSD-style license and third-party notices in its source tree.
    The binding tag pins the binary; it does not by itself establish the
    Chromium PDFium source revision or every linked dependency revision.
  libarchive-jni.so — me.zhanghai.android.libarchive:library:1.1.6 (Apache-2.0):
    https://github.com/zhanghai/libarchive-android/tree/v1.1.6
    See .gitmodules and library/src/main/jni at that tag. Native source pins:
    libarchive 3.8.1 (BSD-2-Clause) https://github.com/libarchive/libarchive/tree/9525f90ca4bd14c7b335e2f8c84a4607b0af6bdf
    bzip2 (bzip2-1.0.6 license) https://gitlab.com/bzip2/bzip2/-/tree/6a8690fc8d26c815e798c588f796eabe9d684cf0
    xz / liblzma (0BSD) https://github.com/tukaani-project/xz/tree/a522a226545730551f7e7c2685fab27cf567746c
    LZ4 (BSD-2-Clause for the library) https://github.com/lz4/lz4/tree/ebb370ca83af193212df4dcbadcc5d87bc0de2f0
    Zstandard (BSD-3-Clause option) https://github.com/facebook/zstd/tree/f8745da6ff1ad1e7bab384bd1f9d742439278e99
    Mbed TLS (Apache-2.0 option) https://github.com/Mbed-TLS/mbedtls/tree/2fc8413bfcb51354c8e679141b17b3f1a5942561
  libandroidx.graphics.path.so — AndroidX Graphics Path, Apache-2.0:
    https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/support/+/androidx-main/graphics/graphics-path/
    Use the resolved graphics-path coordinate in ANDROID-RUNTIME-SOURCES.txt
